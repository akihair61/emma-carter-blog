<p>Let me be honest with you: getting a haircut in a foreign country is terrifying. I've had disasters in Bali, a questionable trim in Chiang Mai, and one truly unforgettable catastrophe in Vietnam that I still can't talk about. So when I moved to Bangkok, I spent three months doing research before I let anyone near my hair.</p>

<p>That research led me to <strong>Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki</strong>, tucked away on the 2nd floor of Ariston Hotel on Sukhumvit Soi 24 — just three minutes on foot from Emporium, right off Phrom Phong BTS station.</p>

<h2>First Impressions</h2>

<p>The salon has that calm, precise energy you associate with Japanese beauty culture. No loud pop music, no chaos. Just clean lines, professional staff, and the quiet hum of focus. Aki, the owner, trained in Japan and it shows — in the way she consults, the way she works, and most importantly, in the results.</p>

<h2>The Cut</h2>

<p>I came in for a cut and blow-dry. Aki spent a good 10 minutes just talking to me about my hair history, my natural texture, and what I actually wanted versus what I said I wanted (those are often different things). She adjusted her technique for my fine European hair and the result was the best haircut I've had in Southeast Asia. No contest.</p>

<h2>Who Is This Salon For?</h2>

<p>If you're Japanese, you'll feel right at home. The salon serves a strong Japanese expat clientele and the communication in Japanese is flawless. But as a British expat, I was made to feel completely welcome — the team speaks enough English to communicate clearly and Aki herself is warm and personable.</p>

<p>If you're in Bangkok and care about your hair, <strong>Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki</strong> is worth the trip to Phrom Phong. I've been three times now and I'm not going anywhere else.</p>
