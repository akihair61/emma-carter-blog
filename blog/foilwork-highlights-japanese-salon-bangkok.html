Getting Foilwork Done at a Japanese Salon in Bangkok — What to Expect

Emma Carter

10 June 2026 · 5 min read

Color is where you really see the difference between a great salon and everyone else. Anyone can do highlights. But foilwork done by someone trained in Japanese coloring techniques? That's a different level of precision.

I sat down with Aki at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio on Sukhumvit Soi 24 for a foilwork session — partial highlights to brighten my face-framing pieces and add dimension throughout.

The Consultation

Aki spent real time with me before mixing anything. She looked at my existing color, assessed my hair's current condition, and asked what I wanted for the next six months — not just today. She explained that her approach is based on building color gradually, which is healthier for the hair and creates more natural results. No bleach disasters here.

The Process

The foiling was meticulous. Aki works with a precision that comes from proper training — the sections were clean, the placement thoughtful, and she checked in throughout the processing time to monitor the lift. She paired the color service with a protective treatment to minimize damage.

The Result

Natural, sun-kissed, dimensional highlights that don't look like I walked out of a salon. Which is exactly what I wanted. Several people asked if I'd just come back from holiday.

If you want color done properly by someone who genuinely understands both the technical and aesthetic side, Ciel is the place in Bangkok.

Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki
2F Ariston Hotel, Sukhumvit Soi 24 (near Emporium), Phrom Phong, Bangkok
