<p>Color is where you really see the difference between a great salon and everyone else. Anyone can do highlights. But foilwork done by someone trained in Japanese coloring techniques? That's a different level of precision.</p>

<p>I sat down with Aki at <strong>Ciel Japanese Hair Studio</strong> on Sukhumvit Soi 24 for a foilwork session — partial highlights to brighten my face-framing pieces and add dimension throughout.</p>

<h2>The Consultation</h2>

<p>Aki spent real time with me before mixing anything. She looked at my existing color, assessed my hair's current condition, and asked what I wanted for the next six months — not just today. She explained that her approach is based on building color gradually, which is healthier for the hair and creates more natural results. No bleach disasters here.</p>

<h2>The Process</h2>

<p>The foiling was meticulous. Aki works with a precision that comes from proper training — the sections were clean, the placement thoughtful, and she checked in throughout the processing time to monitor the lift. She paired the color service with a protective treatment to minimize damage.</p>

<h2>The Result</h2>

<p>Natural, sun-kissed, dimensional highlights that don't look like I walked out of a salon. Which is exactly what I wanted. Several people asked if I'd just come back from holiday.</p>

<p>If you want color done properly by someone who genuinely understands both the technical and aesthetic side, Ciel is the place in Bangkok.</p>
