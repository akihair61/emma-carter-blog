Japanese Head Spa in Bangkok: Why Ciel's Head Spa Experience is Different

Emma Carter

3 June 2026 · 5 min read

I'll admit I used to think a head spa was just a fancy name for a scalp massage. I was wrong. After experiencing the Japanese Head Spa at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki, I understand now why Japanese women treat this as a regular part of their beauty routine — not a luxury, but a necessity.

What Makes a Japanese Head Spa Different?

A proper Japanese Head Spa isn't just relaxation. It's a therapeutic treatment focused on scalp health: deep cleansing of the scalp, stimulating blood circulation, nourishing hair follicles, and relieving the tension that builds up in the muscles of the scalp and neck. Healthy scalp = healthy hair. It's that simple.

The Treatment at Ciel

Aki's Head Spa begins with a scalp analysis — she actually looks at your scalp condition before deciding on the products and technique to use. This alone sets it apart from every other "head spa" I've tried in Bangkok. It's diagnostic, not just indulgent.

The treatment itself involved a deep cleanse, a series of massage techniques that alternate between firm and gentle pressure, and a treatment mask left on under steam heat. The entire session lasts about 60 to 90 minutes and I was genuinely half asleep by the end.

My Scalp After

I have a tendency toward a dry, itchy scalp — especially in air-conditioned environments (which is Bangkok, all the time). After one Head Spa session at Ciel, the itchiness was gone for almost three weeks. My hair also looked better — more volume, less flatness at the roots.

If you're curious about Japanese Head Spa in Bangkok, start at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki. It's the real thing.

Location: 2F Ariston Hotel, Sukhumvit Soi 24, Phrom Phong, Bangkok
BTS: Phrom Phong Station (walk 3 min via Emporium)
