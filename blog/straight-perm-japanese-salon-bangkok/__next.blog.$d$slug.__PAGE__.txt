<p>Japanese straight perm — known in Japan as 縮毛矯正 (shukumou kyousei) — is considered the gold standard for permanently straightening hair. It's a chemical process that restructures the hair's internal bonds to create silky, straight results that last for months. Done right, it's transformative. Done wrong, it's catastrophic.</p>

<p>In Bangkok, finding someone who truly knows how to do this treatment properly is difficult. Which is why <strong>Ciel Japanese Hair Studio by Aki</strong> in Phrom Phong stands out.</p>

<h2>Why Japanese Training Matters Here</h2>

<p>Straight perm requires an understanding of hair chemistry, precise timing, and the right technique for each hair type. Aki trained in Japan specifically in these chemical services, and it shows in the way she approaches the treatment — methodically, conservatively, and always prioritizing hair health over speed.</p>

<h2>My Experience</h2>

<p>I went in with naturally wavy hair that goes frizzy in Bangkok's humidity (so, every day). The consultation included a strand test and an honest conversation about what was realistic for my hair's condition. Aki didn't oversell — she explained the process, the maintenance, and what to expect.</p>

<p>The treatment took about three hours. Results: smooth, straight, shiny hair with zero frizz. And crucially — my hair didn't feel damaged or straw-like afterward. The condition was maintained.</p>

<h2>Maintenance and Longevity</h2>

<p>Aki recommends Tokio treatment to maintain hair health between straight perm sessions. With proper care, results last six months or more. She also provides aftercare guidance in both English and Japanese.</p>

<p>If you're in Bangkok and considering a straight perm, don't cut corners. Go to someone who was trained in Japan to do it properly.</p>
