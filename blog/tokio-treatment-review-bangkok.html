Tokio Treatment in Bangkok: My Honest Review at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio

Emma Carter

28 May 2026 · 5 min read

My hair has been through a lot. Years of highlights, a few ill-advised bleach sessions, and the brutal Bangkok humidity have left it looking more like straw than the glossy waves I was born with. So when Aki at Ciel Japanese Hair Studio mentioned Tokio Inkarami treatment, I was skeptical but desperate enough to try.

What Is Tokio Inkarami?

Tokio Inkarami is a professional hair treatment from Japan that works by penetrating the hair cortex and rebuilding damaged protein bonds from the inside out. It's not a surface-level conditioner — it's a structural repair. The brand is widely used in Japanese salons and is considered one of the best in the industry.

The Experience at Ciel

The treatment took about 90 minutes. Aki applied the product in sections, working it through methodically. There was a heat process involved, and then a rinse and blow-out. The whole experience felt incredibly professional — every step was explained, the timing was precise, nothing felt rushed.

The Results

I'm not going to be dramatic about this: my hair felt different the moment she blow-dried it. Softer. Smoother. Actually shiny. Three weeks later, it still feels significantly better than before. The texture has improved, the frizz is reduced, and my highlights look more vibrant against the healthier base.

Is It Worth the Price?

Yes. If you have color-treated or chemically processed hair and you're living in or visiting Bangkok, one Tokio treatment at Ciel is worth every baht. I'm already booked for a follow-up session next month.
